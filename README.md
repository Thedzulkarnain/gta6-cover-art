# VICE PRESS — GTA 6-style cover art studio

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01.svg)](https://make.fun)
[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01/thedzulkarnain/gta6-cover-art/rank.svg)](https://make.fun)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01/thedzulkarnain/gta6-cover-art/reward.svg)](https://make.fun)

Turn your own photos into GTA 6-style multi-panel cover art, entirely offline in your browser. Add photos (or load the four samples), pick one of 8 layouts, choose one of 10 grades — photographic Natural by default, or nine posterized grades including Comic Ink — then fine-tune contrast, saturation, grain and vignette, edit the review quote, title, script subtitle and tagline, pick a Seal or Starburst badge, and export a PNG in one of 5 sizes (1920×1080, 1080×1350, 1080×1080, 1080×1920, 1200×628) or all five at once with Export All Sizes.

The cover keeps movie-poster polish: a five-star review quote above the title, a 3D-extruded sunset-gradient title, a neon script subtitle, a palm-tree and half-sun divider strip, a tagline bar, and a film footer with a VP monogram, catalogue number and a drawn barcode. Panels are draggable (drag to move the photo, scroll to zoom) with undo and redo.

Run it locally with `node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080. Photos never leave your machine — there is no upload, no server and no watermark. Fonts: Anton and Pacifico, both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see `app/fonts/`).
