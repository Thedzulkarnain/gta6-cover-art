# VICE PRESS — GTA 6-style cover art studio

[![gta6-cover-art-01](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01.svg)](https://make.fun/competition/gta6-cover-art-01)
[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01/thedzulkarnain/gta6-cover-art/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/competition/gta6-cover-art-01)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01/thedzulkarnain/gta6-cover-art/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/competition/gta6-cover-art-01)

VICE PRESS turns your own photos into GTA 6-style multi-panel cover art,
entirely offline in the browser. Add photos (or load the four samples),
pick one of 8 layouts and one of 10 grades, tune contrast, saturation,
grain and vignette, edit the review quote, title, script subtitle and
tagline, choose a seal or starburst badge, then export a PNG at any of
5 sizes — or all five at once with EXPORT ALL SIZES.

The default Natural grade is photographic: a warm sunset grade over real
photos, like real GTA VI key art. The other nine grades posterize the
photo and gradient-map it (Sunset, Neon, Golden, Miami, Vice Purple,
Teal Noir, Bubblegum, Mono Ink, Comic Ink), with optional ink outlines
(Sobel edges), halftone dots and poster ink. Every cover carries the
movie-poster polish: a five-star review quote above the title, a 3D
extruded gradient title, a palm-tree divider strip, a tagline bar, and a
film footer with catalogue number, monogram and a drawn barcode.

Run it with `node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080. No upload,
no watermark, no network requests. Fonts: Anton and Pacifico, vendored
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see app/fonts/).
